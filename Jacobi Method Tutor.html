How to enter your answer
Type thisFor
3 or -2Whole numbers. Use the minus key for negatives.
7/3 or -5/2Fractions: top number, a slash, then the bottom number.
2.33333 or 0.5Decimals, with a period as the decimal point.
  • Write a mixed number as one fraction: type 3/2, not 1 1/2.
  • Don’t use brackets, units, or words. Spaces around the slash are fine.
  • The bottom number can’t be 0.
  • A fraction must be exactly right. A decimal is accepted if it’s within 0.00001 of the answer, so 2.33333 works for 7/3 but 2.33 does not.
  • You don’t need to simplify, but 14/6 and 7/3 both count as correct.
